# Ticket listing red flags

High-risk signals (any one is serious):
- Payment only by Zelle, Venmo "friends and family", Cash App, gift cards, wire or crypto (no buyer protection)
- Seller refuses PayPal Goods & Services, credit card, or an official transfer
- Sends screenshots or PDFs instead of an official transfer (Ticketmaster, Telecharge, TodayTix)
- Seat doesn't exist at that theater (bad section, row too high, box seats at a theater with none)
- Price far below face value for a popular show, especially "sold out" shows

Medium-risk signals:
- Urgency or pressure ("must sell today", "someone else is paying now")
- Emotional story used to rush you (emergency, sick relative)
- Brand-new account, no history, no mutual connections, stolen-looking photos
- Wants to move off the platform to text or email immediately
- Vague details (no date, section or row)

Reassuring signals:
- Price at or near face value, with a clear reason for selling
- Offers an official in-app transfer and protected payment
- Specific, plausible seat details that match the theater
- Established member of a group, with vouches from others

Rating guide: low = mostly reassuring, high = any high-risk signal or several medium ones.
